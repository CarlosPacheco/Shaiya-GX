# Group loot

GX replaces the original item divisions with one rule for parties and raids.

## Who earns the loot

The loot of a monster belongs to whoever dealt it the **most total damage**
before it died. The last hit does not decide it, and neither does who held the
monster's attention. Damage from members of one party or raid counts together
against a lone player or another group.

## Where it goes

- A group's items go **straight into a member's bag**. They do not fall on the
  ground. Only members on the monster's map and within 100 units of it take
  part, alive or dead.
- Gold is still split evenly among those members.
- Quest items fall on the ground as usual.
- A lone player's loot falls on the ground, reserved for them as before.

## The round

1. The first item goes to a random member in reach.
2. Each next item goes to a random member who has not received one yet.
3. When everyone in reach has received one, a new round starts.
4. A member whose bag is full is skipped for that item and keeps their turn.
   If an item no longer fits when it arrives, it falls where the monster died.

Everyone else in the group sees a chat line, "<name> attained <item>".

## Joining, leaving and disconnecting

- Someone who joins while a round is under way waits for the **next** round.
- Someone who leaves or disconnects keeps their exact place for **10 minutes**.
  If they come back in time, they are served or waiting exactly as before.
  After that they count as new members.
- The memory lasts as long as the group does. A new group starts with a fresh
  random draw.
