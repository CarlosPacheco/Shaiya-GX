# GM commands

The maintained GM guide has moved to the
[GitHub Wiki — GM commands](https://github.com/Spelunkern/Shaiya-GX/wiki/GM-Commands).
It covers authority, syntax, examples, limits, transient spawns and protocol.

The editable documentation source is [docs/wiki/gm-commands.md](wiki/gm-commands.md).

A GM can preview rain with `/weather 1` or snow with `/weather 2`. The preview lasts ten minutes and applies only to their session.
